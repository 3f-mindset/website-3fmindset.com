Turn review into a tempering plan

Choose one area. Define three strikes.

Broad intentions do not reshape a life. Each action needs a point of contact, a time or trigger, and a specific result.

Strike 01

Choose the contact

The conversation, boundary, routine, repair, or focused block that matters now.

Strike 02

Fix the trigger

Give the action a day, time, place, or event that makes execution clear.

Strike 03

Name the formation

Define what this repeated action is intended to strengthen in you or your life.

  • Protect one hour of focused work
  • Have the delayed conversation
  • Put the phone away during dinner
  • Review money at a fixed time
  • Train on scheduled days
  • Repair a broken promise
  • Strengthen one boundary
  • Give one role full presence

Why tempering matters

Strength must be able to hold.

Steel can become hard and still remain weak. Too much force without recovery creates brittleness. Short-term output rises while the structure that carries the work begins to fail.

Tempering helps you protect the conditions that allow you to return. It separates discipline from self-destruction and keeps pressure useful.
