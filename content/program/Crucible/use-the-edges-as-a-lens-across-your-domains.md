## Use the Edges as a lens across your domains

Keep **life domains** and **Edges** as two different lenses in your workshop:

- **Domains** answer: *Where in your life are you looking?*
- **Edges** answer: *What quality of character does this area call for—and how well is it developed or balanced in you?*

The book frames the Edges as capacities that work together, rather than isolated strengths to maximize. See [The Geometry of the Blade](https://github.com/3f-mindset/book/blob/main/Draft/8%20The%20Geometry%20of%20the%20Blade%20%5B7%5D.md) and the individual chapters: [Direction](https://github.com/3f-mindset/book/blob/main/Draft/9%20The%20Edge%20of%20Direction%20%5B8%5D.md), [Craft](https://github.com/3f-mindset/book/blob/main/Draft/10%20The%20Edge%20of%20Craft%20%5B9%5D.md), [Alignment](https://github.com/3f-mindset/book/blob/main/Draft/11%20The%20Edge%20of%20Alignment%20%5B10%5D.md), [Stewardship](https://github.com/3f-mindset/book/blob/main/Draft/12%20The%20Edge%20of%20Stewardship%20%5B11%5D.md), [Adaptation](https://github.com/3f-mindset/book/blob/main/Draft/13%20The%20Edge%20of%20Adaptation%20%5B12%5D.md), [Service](https://github.com/3f-mindset/book/blob/main/Draft/14%20The%20Edge%20of%20Service%20%5B13%5D.md), [Legacy](https://github.com/3f-mindset/book/blob/main/Draft/15%20The%20Edge%20of%20Legacy%20%5B14%5D.md), [Integration](https://github.com/3f-mindset/book/blob/main/Draft/16%20The%20Edge%20of%20Integration%20%5B15%5D.md), and [Expression](https://github.com/3f-mindset/book/blob/main/Draft/17%20The%20Edge%20of%20Expression%20%5B16%5D.md).

### Check the Edges within each priority domain

After you choose a domain and describe your vision, reflect:

1. What does this domain ask of you?
2. Which Edge or Edges would help you meet that responsibility well?
3. Which Edge are you already relying on heavily here?
4. Could that strength become an overused Edge or blind spot for you in this domain?
5. Which Edge needs more development, or needs to be balanced by another?
6. What would you do differently this week if you brought that Edge to this domain?

For example, in **work & contribution**, you might have plenty of craft and direction but notice that you neglect service or integration. Do not prescribe a “right” Edge; use the questions to see what your domain calls forth and what balance your vision requires.

### Test your vision across domains

Once you have a draft vision for several domains, ask yourself: **Do these visions form a coherent whole—or does one domain’s vision depend on neglecting another?** Which Edges might help you reconcile the tension? Direction may clarify what you want to prioritize; adaptation may help you fit the plan to real constraints; stewardship may surface responsibilities you cannot simply ignore. You do not need equal effort in every domain; look for coherence and sustainability across your overall direction.

Treat this as a reflection layer—not another set of domains or a required scorecard. Keep your workshop focused on choosing a handful of life domains and forming a vision in each, then use the nine Edges to examine how you will embody that vision and where you may be out of balance.
