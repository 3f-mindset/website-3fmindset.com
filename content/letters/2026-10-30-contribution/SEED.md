# Steady Burn Week 44: Contribution

- **Phase:** 6 — Install the Furnace
- **Release:** 2026-10-30

## Core idea

Strength becomes hollow when it never leaves the man who built it. Contribution directs capability toward family, community, and people who need a steady hand.

## Empowering action

Choose one concrete contribution and complete it without seeking recognition.

## Steps

1. Identify a person or place within your reach that needs help.
2. Match a real need with a strength, resource, or hour you can offer.
3. Set a date and finish the contribution.
4. Reflect on what service revealed about your capacity.

## Worksheet outcome

Define the need, contribution, delivery date, and evidence of completion.

## Journal prompt

> Where could the strength I am building become useful to someone besides me?
