# STRUCTURE: From Vision to Action Steps

This worksheet is not a prettier to-do list. It is a roadmap for one meaningful goal. Complete it once, schedule the first action, and bring it to your weekly review.

## S: Specify the Goal

Write one outcome in specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound language.

Avoid: “get life together,” “be productive,” or other fog.

Better: “Launch the side-business website by March 1, with design complete by January 31 and copy complete by February 15.”

Finish when the goal has a deadline and a clear definition of success.

## T: Track Milestones

Work backward from the deadline. Name the quarter-way, halfway, and final milestones. Under each milestone, write the evidence that will show it is complete.

Avoid: choosing tasks because they are quick or because someone else asked loudly.

Better: choose the work tied to a real obligation, goal, or cost of delay.

Finish when each milestone is an observable outcome with a date.

## R: Route the Actions

Under the first milestone, list the actions or habits required. Put the first action into a real calendar block with a start, end, and location.

Avoid: “I’ll get to it sometime tomorrow.”

Better: “Tuesday 9:00–9:45, desk, draft the first three website sections.”

Finish when the first action is scheduled and the following actions have owners or dates.

## U: Use the Weekly Review

Choose a weekly review time. Record what moved, what stalled, and what the evidence says. Adjust the action, timeline, or milestone when needed.

Avoid: carrying every task forward unchanged.

Better: “Delete old research,” “delegate invoice check to Sam by Wednesday,” or “batch calls from 4:00–4:30.”

Finish when the next review is scheduled and the plan can respond to reality.

## Complete and Tell the Truth

Complete the first action this week. Record what happened without performance theater. Do not use the review to punish yourself. Use it to improve the next decision.

The finish condition is simple: one written goal, dated milestones, a completed first action, and a recurring weekly review.
