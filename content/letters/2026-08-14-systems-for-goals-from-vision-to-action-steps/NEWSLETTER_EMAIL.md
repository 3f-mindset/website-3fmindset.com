## Subject
SteadyBurn: Turn Your Big Goal Into a Plan You Can Start Today

## Body
This week, one big goal stops living in your head and becomes a written plan with a deadline, dated milestones, and a first task you can complete today.

You have probably felt it before. You know the goal. You know why it matters. You sit down with a notebook, see a blank page, and close it before you can name the next step. The goal is not stuck because you are lazy. It is stuck because it has no shape. A goal with no plan is a wish, and a wish waits for motivation, a free weekend, or the right mood. When work, family, or health takes the first slot in your week, the goal slips again.

The weekly contextual essay follows that stuck feeling from the blank page to the first task. It begins with a goal that has a number and a date but no next step, then shows why heat without a path leaks and why a goal needs containment. It moves from the stuck feeling into the shape of a plan: one goal, dated milestones, and the first task for the week.

The essay then explains the weekly check that keeps the plan alive. It shows how a short review time turns a one-time plan into a working system, how numbers reveal whether the plan is too hard or too easy, and how a missed week can be repaired instead of treated as a restart. That is why this worksheet matters now: it gives the goal a place in your week before motivation fades.

The lesson PDF is attached. The worksheet is included in this packet. The public letter is the weekly contextual essay linked below. The GPT coach is your support for getting through the worksheet without overthinking it. The worksheet is the central execution artifact for the week, and the rest of the packet exists to help you complete it.

The worksheet is not a place for inspiration. It is a place for decisions. Use it to turn your goal from a cloud into a project. By the time you finish, you should be able to say: “Here is the goal. Here is the deadline. Here are the milestones. Here are the dates. Here are the actions. Here is the first task I will do this week.”

In 7 days, you will have one written goal plan with a deadline, three milestones, and the first task completed. You do not need to give up your job, your family time, or your budget. You need to give the goal a fixed place in your week.

The worksheet will produce a plan you can actually follow. You will assign one goal, unpack it into milestones, date each milestone, insert the actions into your week, and set a weekly review time. That process moves you from “I do not know where to start” to “I have a first task done and a next task scheduled.” It also moves you from “I am behind” to “I can show my progress with dates and numbers.” This is where your broader weekly transformation begins: the goal stops competing with every other task and becomes part of your normal life.
