## Title
Turn Your Big Goal Into a Plan You Can Start Today

## Subtitle
This week’s topic is systems for goals. You will learn how to move from a vague dream to dated milestones, weekly actions, and a simple review that keeps you moving.

## Promise
In 7 days, you will have one written goal plan with a deadline, three milestones, and the first task completed, without giving up your job, your family time, or your budget.

## Motivations
- Finish a big goal that has been stuck in your head for months.
- Know exactly what to do this week for the goal.
- See proof of progress with numbers, dates, and completed tasks.
- Stop starting over every time life gets busy.
- Build a routine that keeps the goal moving even on hard days.

## Challenges & Sacrifices
- You have a goal but no written plan, so you do not know the next step.
- You try to work on the goal only when you feel motivated, and motivation fades.
- You skip the goal when work, family, or health takes the first slot in your week.
- You set a big deadline but no smaller deadlines, so you fall behind quietly.
- You give up time, money, or rest to chase the goal, but you do not track if it is working.

## Frustrations
- You feel stuck because the goal is still too big to touch.
- You feel behind because you cannot say what you completed this week.
- You feel unsure because you do not know if your plan is too hard or too easy.
- You feel scattered because the goal competes with every other task.
- You feel like a quitter because you start, stop, and restart the same goal.

## Transformations
- From “I do not know where to start” to “I have a first task done and a next task scheduled.”
- From “I am behind” to “I can show my progress with dates and numbers.”
- From “I do not know if this is working” to “I review my plan weekly and fix what is off track.”
- From “my goal gets pushed aside” to “my goal has a fixed place in my week.”
- From “I keep restarting” to “I keep moving with a simple system.”

## Choice
You can keep living with the frustrations above. You can keep hoping the goal will happen when you feel ready. That is the hard way. Or you can choose to take action now. You pick one goal, write a plan, set dates, and complete the first step this week. That starts the path to a goal you can actually finish.

## Actionable VERB
AUDIT

## Model
Use the AUDIT model to move from stuck to steady.

A: Assign one goal. Pick one big goal that matters to you. Write it in clear terms with a number and a deadline. Example: “Save $5,000 by December 31” or “Run a 10K by June 1.” If the goal is about a relationship, write what success looks like, like “Call my brother every Sunday for 3 months.” This step stops you from chasing many goals at once.

U: Unpack the goal into milestones. Break the goal into 3 to 5 smaller targets. Ask what must be true at the halfway point and at the quarter points. For the savings goal, set milestones like $1,000 by March 31, $2,500 by June 30, and $4,000 by September 30. For the run, set milestones like run 3 miles by April 1 and run 5 miles by May 1. This step turns a big dream into checkpoints.

D: Date each milestone. Put a deadline on every milestone. A milestone without a date is just a wish. Write the date next to each target. Then work backward to the present. Ask what must be done this week to hit the first milestone. This step gives you a clear path from today to the finish line.

I: Insert the actions into your week. Under each milestone, list the tasks or habits you need. For savings, that may be “Move $500 on the 1st” and “Eat out once a week.” For the run, that may be “Run 3 times a week” and “Rest on planned days.” Put these tasks into your calendar or weekly plan. This step makes the goal part of your normal life, not an extra wish.

T: Track and adjust weekly. Set a short review time, like Sunday evening. Check what you completed, what you missed, and what the numbers show. If you are on track, keep going. If you are off track, change one action or move one milestone. This step completes the lesson by turning your plan into a living system that keeps you moving.

## Action Steps Summary
1. Write the Goal. Put one goal on paper with a number and a deadline. This gives you a clear target and stops vague dreaming.
2. Build the Roadmap. Break the goal into dated milestones and weekly actions. This gives you a path you can follow without guessing.
3. Run the Weekly Audit. Review progress, check the numbers, and adjust one thing if needed. This keeps you honest and prevents small slips from becoming big delays.

## FAQ
- “Why do I keep setting goals but not finishing them?” Because you have a wish, not a system. You need a written plan, dated milestones, and a weekly review.
- “How do I know if my goal is realistic?” Check if you can name the first task and the first milestone. If you cannot, the goal is too big or too vague.
- “What if I miss a week?” Do not restart from zero. Update your log, find the missed action, and put it back into the next week.
- “Do I need a fancy app to track my goal?” No. You need a place to write the goal, milestones, dates, and weekly progress. A notebook or simple spreadsheet works.
- “How do I keep from giving up when life gets busy?” Put the goal actions into your calendar like appointments. Then review them weekly and adjust the plan instead of dropping it.
