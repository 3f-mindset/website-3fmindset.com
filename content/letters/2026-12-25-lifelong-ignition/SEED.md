# Steady Burn Week 52: Lifelong Ignition

- **Phase:** 7 — Name Your Flame
- **Release:** 2026-12-25

## Core idea

The year ends, but the forge remains. Lifelong ignition is the vow to keep returning to the practices, standards, and mission that made the transformation possible.

## Empowering action

Write a twelve-month continuation vow and schedule the first review of the next year.

## Steps

1. Name the practices that must not disappear after Week 52.
2. Define the next season’s mission and first measurable target.
3. Write the promise you make to yourself and those you lead.
4. Put the first quarterly review on the calendar now.

## Worksheet outcome

Create a continuation vow, non-negotiables list, next target, and first review date.

## Journal prompt

> What will I do when the structure of this program is gone and only my own standards remain?
