## Model
FUEL model:

F - Find the one goal that needs outside pressure.
Context: You may have many goals. Pick one where a nudge helps.
Instructions: Write down one goal or habit you are struggling with. Make it small and clear. Example: "I will send three job applications this week." Decide why it matters.

U - Use a simple check-in method.
Context: You need a way to report.
Instructions: Choose one method: partner, group, coach, family member, app, or public post. Pick a frequency: daily text, weekly call, or Sunday report. Keep it short.

E - Enter a clear agreement.
Context: A vague promise is easy to skip.
Instructions: Tell the person or group exactly what you will report, when, and what happens if you miss. Example: "I will send my gym log every Sunday. If I do not, call me out." Set a start date.

L - Log and live the first check-in.
Context: The system works only when you use it.
Instructions: Do the first check-in on time. Report honestly. If you miss, say so. If it is two-way, help the other person too. Finish the lesson by keeping the first check-in and planning the next one.

## Action Steps Summary
1. Pick Your Pressure Point
Choose one goal and one check-in method. This gives you a clear target and a simple way to stay visible.
2. Set the Check-In
Agree on the time, how it will happen, and the small cost or reward. This makes the next step hard to skip and keeps the effort honest.
3. Report and Repeat
Send the first update on time and plan the next one. This builds proof, momentum, and a habit that can survive busy weeks.

## FAQ
- Why do I always start strong and then fade?
You may be relying only on memory and mood. Add a scheduled check-in so the next step has a date and a person who knows.
- Do I need a perfect partner to stay on track?
No. You need one person or tool that will ask for a short update. A friend, family member, group, or app can work.
- What if I feel embarrassed when I miss?
Misses are facts, not shame. Report the miss, fix the plan, and keep the next check-in.
- How much time does this take?
A few minutes. A short text, a 10-minute call, or a weekly log is enough to create pressure and support.
- Will this feel like someone is watching me?
It should feel like support with healthy pressure. You asked for the check-in because it helps you keep your word.
