# Model

## P — Pause the Pattern

Stop reacting automatically. Look honestly at the situation that keeps failing.

Ask:

- What result am I trying to force?
- What strategy keeps failing?
- What signs have I ignored?

You cannot adapt if you refuse to admit the current approach is broken.

## I — Inspect the Friction

Study the resistance instead of fighting it emotionally.

Write down:

- What keeps slowing progress?
- What changed recently?
- What part of the process creates the most stress?

Friction is feedback. Treat it like information.

## V — Visualize New Angles

Brainstorm at least three different ways to solve the problem.

Examples:

- Change your routine
- Change your communication style
- Change your environment
- Change your timing
- Change the order of operations

The goal is not comfort. The goal is movement.

## O — Operate Before Ready

Test a new strategy immediately.

Do not wait for certainty.

Action creates clarity faster than overthinking. A small move today teaches more than another week of analysis.

## T — Track the Response

Pay attention to what improves and what still fails.

Track:

- Energy
- Results
- Stress
- Time
- Consistency

Good adaptation sharpens results while reducing unnecessary resistance.

## S — Sharpen Through Repetition

Keep refining the process.

Adaptation is not a one time event. It becomes a skill through repeated correction.

The men who improve fastest are usually the men least attached to being right.

# Action Steps Summary

## Break

Identify one area where your current strategy keeps failing. Stop defending the old plan and admit where you are stuck.

## Pivot

Choose and test a new approach immediately. Build confidence by proving you can move differently under pressure.

## Refine

Track what works, remove what does not, and keep adjusting until progress becomes consistent again.

# FAQ

## Why do I keep getting stuck even when I work hard?

Because effort alone does not fix a bad strategy. Hard work must be paired with adjustment.

## How do I know when to change my approach?

When the same effort keeps producing the same poor result, it is time to adapt.

## Does adapting mean giving up?

No. Giving up abandons the goal. Adapting changes the path to reach it.

## Why is changing direction so uncomfortable?

Because most people connect their identity to being right instead of being effective.

## What if the new strategy fails too?

Then you learn faster and adjust again. Flexibility keeps momentum alive while stubbornness kills it.
