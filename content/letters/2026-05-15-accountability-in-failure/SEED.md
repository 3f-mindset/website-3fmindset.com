# Steady Burn Week 20: Accountability in Failure

- **Phase:** 3 — Navigate the Fire
- **Release:** 2026-05-15

## Core aim

This seed translates the accountability in failure curriculum theme into one direct weekly practice. The work must be concrete, honest, and measurable—not another idea left in a notebook.

## Empowering action

Conduct a no-excuses failure review and take one corrective action within 24 hours.

## Worksheet outcome

Name the current reality, the standard for this week, the first action, and the proof that it was completed.

## Journal prompt

> What can this setback teach me if I refuse to hide from it?
