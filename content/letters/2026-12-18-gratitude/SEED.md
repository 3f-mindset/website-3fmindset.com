# Steady Burn Week 51: Gratitude

- **Phase:** 7 — Name Your Flame
- **Release:** 2026-12-18

## Core idea

Gratitude keeps strength from becoming entitlement. It recognizes the people, sacrifices, and grace that made a man’s progress possible.

## Empowering action

Offer specific thanks to three people who helped shape your year.

## Steps

1. Name the people whose support, truth, labor, or example mattered.
2. Write what they gave and how it changed you.
3. Deliver the thanks directly where possible.
4. Notice what humility requires you to acknowledge.

## Worksheet outcome

Prepare three specific messages of thanks and a record of the support received.

## Journal prompt

> Whose contribution to my life have I benefited from without fully acknowledging?
