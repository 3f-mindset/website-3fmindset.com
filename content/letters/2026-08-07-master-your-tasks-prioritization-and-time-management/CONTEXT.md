## Model
**T - Tally Your Tasks**
Get everything out of your head. Write down every single task you think you need to do, big or small, for work and for home. Don't organize them yet. The goal is to create a complete inventory so nothing is hiding in the back of your mind causing stress.

**R - Rank by Reality**
Look at your complete list and sort every item. Ask two questions: Is it urgent? Is it important? Important tasks move you toward your major goals. Urgent tasks demand immediate attention. Your highest priority tasks are both important and urgent. Your next focus should be on what's important but not urgent.

**I - Isolate Your Priorities**
From your ranked list, choose the top 1-3 tasks that will make the biggest positive impact today. These are your "big rocks." These are your non-negotiables. Write them on a separate, smaller list. This is your real to-do list for the day.

**A - Allocate Your Time**
Open your calendar and schedule blocks of time to work on your 1-3 priority tasks. Treat these blocks like critical appointments you cannot miss. Schedule them for when you have the most energy, usually earlier in the day.

**G - Guard Your Focus**
When a scheduled time block begins, eliminate all distractions. Put your phone on silent and out of sight. Close unnecessary browser tabs. Let others know you are in a focus session. Your only job during this time is to work on the single task you scheduled.

**E - Evaluate and End**
At the end of the day, review your list of 1-3 priorities. Check off what you completed. If something is unfinished, decide if it carries over to tomorrow's priority list. This daily review builds momentum and helps you see that you are making real progress.

## Action Steps Summary
**1. Rank Your Reality**
Dump all your tasks onto a master list, then sort them by what's truly important versus what's just screaming for attention. This gives you immediate clarity on where your effort should go.

**2. Isolate and Allocate**
Pick your top three "must-do" tasks for the day and schedule them on your calendar like appointments. This commits you to working on what matters most before the day gets away from you.

**3. Guard and Evaluate**
Eliminate all distractions during your scheduled focus time to ensure you get the work done. Reviewing your progress at the end of the day provides a sense of accomplishment and helps you plan for tomorrow.

## FAQ
**Why am I so busy all day but feel like I got nothing done?**
You are likely confusing activity with accomplishment. Your day is filled with reacting to urgent but unimportant tasks, like emails and minor requests, instead of proactively working on the tasks that truly move you forward.

**How can I ever get to my big goals when I'm buried in daily chores?**
You must intentionally set aside and protect time for your big goals *first*. By scheduling your most important tasks like appointments, you ensure they get your best energy before the day's small chores take over.

**My to-do list is a mile long. Where do I even start?**
Start by asking, "If I could only do three things today, which ones would make the biggest difference?" This simple question cuts through the noise and instantly reveals your top priorities.

**I get distracted so easily. How do I stay focused?**
Focus isn't just about willpower; it's about creating an environment for it. By scheduling a specific time block and removing all possible distractions—phone, email, other people—you make it easy to stay on task.

**Is it bad to say "no" to new requests when I'm already swamped?**
It is essential. Every time you say "yes" to a low-priority request, you are implicitly saying "no" to your own important goals. Protecting your priorities is a key skill of effective people.
