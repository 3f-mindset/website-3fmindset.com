# Steady Burn Week 28: Values Alignment

- **Phase:** 4 — Inspect and Refine
- **Release:** 2026-07-10

## Core aim

Find one gap between a stated value and behavior, then close it with a visible action.

## Empowering action

Choose one value you claim to live by and make its next behavioral proof unmistakable.

## Worksheet outcome

Name the value, the gap, the corrective action, and the evidence of alignment.

## Journal prompt

> Where am I asking others to believe a value I am not living?
