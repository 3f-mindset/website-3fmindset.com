Topic: Week 28, Values Alignment Check

## Title

Stop Betraying What You Say Matters

## Subtitle

Your values are not proven by what you believe. They are proven by what your daily choices repeat.

## Promise

Close 1 clear gap between a stated value and a daily behavior this week, without overhauling your life, adding more pressure, or chasing motivation.

## Motivations

1. Live with more consistency between words and actions.
2. Make better decisions without constant second guessing.
3. Build self-respect through visible follow-through.
4. Reduce the quiet stress of knowing you are off track.
5. Become a man whose values are obvious through his habits.

## Challenges & Sacrifices

1. Saying family matters, but giving work and your phone the best of your attention.
2. Saying health matters, but sleeping poorly and eating whatever is fastest.
3. Saying integrity matters, but avoiding hard truths to keep peace.
4. Saying growth matters, but spending free time on comfort instead of learning.
5. Saying discipline matters, but letting mood decide what gets done.

## Frustrations

- You feel like you know better but keep doing the same things.
- You lose trust in your own promises.
- You feel pulled between who you claim to be and how you actually live.
- You keep making excuses for habits that weaken you.
- You feel busy, but not proud of how your days are being spent.

## Transformations

1. From saying values to showing values.
2. From quiet guilt to clean action.
3. From scattered choices to clear standards.
4. From self-doubt to earned self-trust.
5. From drifting through the week to living with proof.

## Choice

You can keep living the hard way: claiming values while your habits tell a different story.

Or you can choose one value, find one gap, and take one action this week that proves your principles are not just words.

## Actionable VERB

**ALIGN**

## Model

### A: Admit the Value

Choose one core value you say matters. Do not pick the safest one. Pick the one that has been bothering you because your behavior has not matched it.

Example: family, health, faith, integrity, discipline, growth, excellence.

### L: List the Evidence

Write two lists.

First, list how you currently support this value.

Second, list how you currently weaken this value.

Do not explain. Do not defend. Just write the facts.

### I: Identify the Gap

Look at both lists and name the one behavior that creates the biggest conflict.

Ask: “If someone watched my life for 30 days, would they believe this value matters to me?”

The gap is where your words and actions disagree.

### G: Give It One Rule

Create one simple rule that closes the gap.

Examples:

No work email after 7 p.m.

Meal prep lunch three days this week.

Read 20 minutes before any screen time.

Tell the truth in the conversation I have been avoiding.

Make the rule clear enough that you can tell if you kept it.

### N: Nail It This Week

Do the rule immediately and repeat it this week.

Do not wait for the perfect mood.

This is not about feeling aligned. It is about acting aligned until your life becomes evidence.

## Action Steps Summary

### 1. Name the Standard

Pick one value that needs inspection. This gives your week a clear target.

### 2. Expose the Drift

Write what supports and weakens that value. This shows the real gap without excuses.

### 3. Prove the Principle

Create one rule and follow it this week. This turns belief into visible action.

## FAQ

### 1. Why do I feel guilty even when life looks fine?

Because part of you knows your actions are not matching your values. Guilt often points to drift.

### 2. Why do I keep breaking promises to myself?

Because the promise is too vague. Turn the value into one clear rule you can follow.

### 3. Why do I feel busy but not proud?

Because activity is not the same as alignment. You may be doing many things while avoiding what matters most.

### 4. How do I know which value to inspect first?

Start with the one you keep defending, explaining, or avoiding. That is usually where the gap is.

### 5. What if I realize one of my values is not really mine?

Then adjust it. A borrowed value creates pressure without purpose. Choose the value you are willing to live.
