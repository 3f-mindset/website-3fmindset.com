# Steady Burn Week 43: Positivity

- **Phase:** 6 — Install the Furnace
- **Release:** 2026-10-23

## Core idea

Positivity is not denial. It is disciplined attention to evidence of progress, opportunity, and what is still within a man’s control.

## Empowering action

Create a daily win log that records three pieces of evidence that you are moving forward.

## Steps

1. Notice the complaint loop you repeat most often.
2. Record one completed action, one lesson, and one source of gratitude each day.
3. Turn one complaint into a controllable next step.
4. Review the log at week’s end.

## Worksheet outcome

Build a seven-day evidence log and complaint-to-action conversion.

## Journal prompt

> What changes when I stop using discouragement as proof that my effort is pointless?
