# Steady Burn Week 7: Define Your Identity

- **Phase:** 1 — Identify the Blade
- **Release:** 2026-02-13

## Core aim

This seed translates the define your identity curriculum theme into one direct weekly practice. The work must be concrete, honest, and measurable—not another idea left in a notebook.

## Empowering action

Draft a present-tense identity statement from your values, strengths, passions, and purpose.

## Worksheet outcome

Name the current reality, the standard for this week, the first action, and the proof that it was completed.

## Journal prompt

> How will this identity change my daily choices?
