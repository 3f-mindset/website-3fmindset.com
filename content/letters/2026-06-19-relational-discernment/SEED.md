# Steady Burn Week 25: Relational Discernment

- **Phase:** 4 — Inspect and Refine
- **Release:** 2026-06-19

## Core aim

This seed translates the relational discernment curriculum theme into one direct weekly practice. The work must be concrete, honest, and measurable—not another idea left in a notebook.

## Empowering action

Assess one relationship honestly and take one action toward health, distance, or repair.

## Worksheet outcome

Name the current reality, the standard for this week, the first action, and the proof that it was completed.

## Journal prompt

> Which relationships strengthen my standards, and which weaken them?
